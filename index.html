Professional Setup

Professional roles

Define the professional roles used across your staff and document requirements — e.g. PT/PTA/OT/COTA/SLP for a therapy staffing company, MD/DO/PA/NP/RN for an urgent care clinic, or Title Agent/Escrow Officer/Notary for a title company. These appear anywhere a role is selected: adding staff, editing a staff member's role, and scoping which document types apply to which roles.

⚠ Licensed or credentialed roles only (e.g. PT, RN, DO, Title Agent) — not job titles or access levels. Someone with no such role should be set to "N/A" on their own profile, not added here as a discipline.

CAQH-exempt disciplines — check any discipline that doesn't need a CAQH profile (e.g. PTA, COTA working under a supervising clinician). Exempt disciplines won't show up as "Missing CAQH ID" anywhere in the app.
